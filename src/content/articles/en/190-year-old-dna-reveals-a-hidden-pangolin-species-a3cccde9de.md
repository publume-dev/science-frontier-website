Researchers used DNA from a nearly 190-year-old museum specimen to confirm a previously unrecognized Asian pangolin species, Manis aurita, which lives in Nepal and Northern India.

DNA from modern Himalayan pangolin samples matched the 1836 type specimen, showing that animals described in 2025 as Manis indoburmanica are actually the same species as Manis aurita, which takes priority under scientific naming rules.

The Himalayan pangolin M. aurita differs from the Chinese pangolin by having a bigger body, longer tail, and smaller ears, and their known ranges do not overlap.
