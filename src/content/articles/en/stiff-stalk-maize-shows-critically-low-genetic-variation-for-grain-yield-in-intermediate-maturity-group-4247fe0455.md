The study analyzed 13 Stiff Stalk and 28 Non-Stiff Stalk inbreds spanning early, intermediate, and late maturity groups, along with 162 single-cross maize hybrids.

When the hybrids were grown at more than 30 locations across the U.S. and Canada, the analysis found critically low genetic variation for grain yield in intermediate-maturity Stiff Stalk inbred lines.

The researchers recommend reintroducing genetic diversity into elite breeding pools, citing the USDA Agricultural Research Service's Germplasm Enhancement of Maize program as one pathway. They frame the depletion as a looming crisis, but the article does not give quantitative measures of the variation loss or its projected impact.
