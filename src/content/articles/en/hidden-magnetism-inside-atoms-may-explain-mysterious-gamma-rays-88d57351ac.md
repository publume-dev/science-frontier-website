Low-energy gamma-ray enhancement has been a puzzle in nuclear decays, as standard nuclear models predict that gamma-ray intensity should decrease with decreasing energy.

The FRIB-led team, including LLNL researchers, separated electric and magnetic decay states in the copper-to-zinc decay and found that only the magnetic transition produced the low-energy gamma-ray enhancement.

The finding indicates that the enhancement is magnetic, not electric, which may lead to improved nuclear models for astrophysics, nuclear energy, national security, and nuclear forensics.
