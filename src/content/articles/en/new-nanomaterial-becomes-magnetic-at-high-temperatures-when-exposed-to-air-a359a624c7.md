Researchers synthesized two new spinel materials composed of silver, chromium, and selenium ions, which are among the first spinels with silver in the 'A' site.

When exposed to air, the original spinel loses silver ions and transforms into a second spinel that retains its magnetic properties up to 400 Kelvin, whereas the original loses magnetism at 152 Kelvin.

This 248 K difference in Curie temperature is the largest change ever documented in Curie temperature.
