WiFi devices transmit beamforming feedback information to optimize signal transmission. This information is usually unencrypted and can be captured by other devices in the vicinity.

Researchers developed a technique that uses standard WiFi devices to analyze this beamforming feedback information, generating images to identify individuals. In a study with 197 participants, the system identified people with almost 100% accuracy regardless of viewing perspective or walking style. After the machine learning model is trained to recognize individuals, the identification process takes only a few seconds.

This finding demonstrates that individuals can be identified by ordinary WiFi infrastructure even without carrying any device, posing a significant privacy risk.
