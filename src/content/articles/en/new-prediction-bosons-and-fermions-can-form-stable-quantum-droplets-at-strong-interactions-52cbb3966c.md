Quantum droplets are self-bound quantum states, but previous theories could only describe systems with weak interactions. The stability of such structures under stronger interactions was previously unexplored.

Monash University researchers predict that in a mixture of bosons and fermions, stable quantum droplets can form when an attractive force is balanced by pressure from the fermions, preventing collapse. This new approach allows exploration of stronger interactions beyond the weak-coupling limit. The predicted droplets could potentially be produced using existing ultracold atom experiments.

If confirmed experimentally, the results suggest a richer variety of quantum phases, including phenomena resembling liquid-gas transitions. However, the prediction is purely theoretical and has not yet been experimentally verified.
