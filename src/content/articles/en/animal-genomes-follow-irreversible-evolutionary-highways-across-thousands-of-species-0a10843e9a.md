Researchers analyzed more than 5,800 publicly available chromosome-scale genomes spanning 4,454 species across 19 animal phyla, and developed a framework called 'evolutionary genome topology' that projects genome diversity onto a single map.

The study found that animal genomes evolve along a limited set of irreversible 'evolutionary highways'. The process 'fusion-with-mixing' is irreversible and leaves a permanent record when chromosomes fuse. The study is published in Science Advances with DOI: 10.1126/sciadv.adz5561.

These findings suggest that genome evolution is not entirely random but follows certain directions and constraints, offering a new perspective on the fundamental rules of genome evolution.
