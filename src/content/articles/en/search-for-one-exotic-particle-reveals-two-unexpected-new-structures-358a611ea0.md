The Y(2175) particle had previously only been observed in electron-positron annihilation. The GlueX experiment aimed to detect it through photoproduction for the first time.

The GlueX Collaboration did not observe Y(2175) but instead discovered two unexpected structures: Y(2240) with a mass around 2.24 GeV at 5σ significance, and X(1830) with a mass around 1.82 GeV at 3σ significance. The study also established an upper limit on the photoproduction of Y(2175).

These surprising findings offer new avenues for exotic hadron research, while also suggesting that Y(2175) may not be produced as expected in photoproduction. Further data will be needed to clarify the nature of the new structures.
