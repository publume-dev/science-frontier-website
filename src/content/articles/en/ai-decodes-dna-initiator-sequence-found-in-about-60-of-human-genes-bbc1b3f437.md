The DNA initiator is a key regulatory element in gene expression, but its specific features had not been fully resolved.

In a study published in Genes and Development, researchers tested about 500,000 different versions of the initiator using high-throughput DNA sequencing, and an AI model decoded the initiator's signature DNA pattern.

About 60% of human genes contain the initiator sequence, potentially offering a new foundation for studying gene regulation, though specific applications require further exploration.
