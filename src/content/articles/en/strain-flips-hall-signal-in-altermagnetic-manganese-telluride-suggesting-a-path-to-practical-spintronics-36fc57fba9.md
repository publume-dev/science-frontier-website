Altermagnets are an emerging class of magnetic materials with near-zero net magnetization but time-reversal symmetry breaking, making them promising for spintronics.

The team applied mechanical strain along one direction on hexagonal MnTe, merging competing magnetic domains into a single domain and clearly resolving the magnetic structure. By tuning the strain, they could flip the sign of the Hall signal without disturbing the underlying magnetic order.

A 1% change in strain has an effect on the Hall signal comparable to changing the temperature by about 150 K, offering a practical control method for devices.
